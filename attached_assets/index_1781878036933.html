How gopack works

What is gopack.now?
gopack is an AI-powered group travel planner. One person creates a trip, shares a link, friends add their wishes and vote — then AI builds a day-by-day itinerary that works for everyone.
Who is it for?
Friend groups, families, couples, and solo travelers looking for a group. Anyone who's tried coordinating a trip over WhatsApp and given up knows exactly why gopack exists.
How does the AI itinerary work?
After everyone adds their wishlist and votes, hit "Build our trip." Claude (Anthropic's AI) searches the web for currently open places and builds a realistic day-by-day plan that weaves in your top wishes.
Is it free?
You can explore public trip itineraries for free. Creating or joining trips requires a free account. Premium features like group-finding and ambassador verification are coming soon.
Can I edit the itinerary?
Yes — every activity has an Edit button. Changes sync in real time to all trip members.
How do I invite friends?
After creating a trip you get a shareable link. Anyone with the link can join. You can optionally set a password for extra privacy.
