How groups get created (no extra work for the user):
The first time you finish a trip with a group of people, the app automatically suggests saving that group. Right after the itinerary is generated or the trip ends, a prompt appears: "Save this group for future trips?" with a suggested name based on the destination ("Lisbon Crew") that the host can rename to whatever they want — "College Friends," "Family," "Work Squad." One tap, done. The group is just a saved list of member references tied to the host's account.
No manual "create a group and add people" flow. The group forms organically from a real trip, which means every saved group represents people who've actually planned together before, not a contact list the user assembled hypothetically.
How groups get used:
When the host creates a new trip, after setting destination/dates/vibes, instead of generating a share link and manually sending it out, they see a step that says "Invite your Pack" with two options: their saved groups listed by name (with member avatars and count), and the existing share link for new people. Tapping a saved group sends a push notification or in-app invite to every member of that group at once. One tap replaces five individual link shares.
What happens on the receiving end:
Each invited member gets a notification: "Sarah invited you to a Barcelona trip with The London Crew." They tap it, land directly in the new trip, and they're in. No link copying, no trip ID entry, no friction. If someone in the group has deleted the app or changed accounts, the invite silently fails for them and the host sees "4 of 5 invited" so they can follow up manually.
Managing groups over time:
Groups aren't static. On the group's detail screen (accessible from profile or dashboard), the host can rename it, remove someone, or add new people from other past trips. If you take a trip with your college friends plus one new person, the app asks "Add Alex to College Friends?" after the trip. Groups grow naturally from trip activity rather than requiring manual curation.
Where it lives in the app:
A small "My Groups" section on the dashboard, below the trip list. Each group shows the name, member avatars, and "last trip: Lisbon, March 2026." Tapping a group shows past trips together and a "Plan a new trip" button that pre-fills the invite step. This also subtly drives repeat usage — seeing "Family — last trip 8 months ago" is a natural nudge to plan another one without the app being pushy about it.
