# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing page (`/`)
- Public community discovery page (`/explore`)
- Public unauthenticated entry points for crawl/indexation hygiene only (`/login`, `/join/:tripId`)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated app routes (`/dashboard`, `/create`, `/trip/**`, `/profile`, `/notifications`)
- User-specific trip content and in-app collaboration flows except where they affect crawlability or index bloat

## Target audience
- Friend groups planning leisure travel together
- Travelers who want collaborative trip planning with AI help

## Primary keywords
- AI group travel planner
- group trip planner
- collaborative trip planning
- AI itinerary planner
