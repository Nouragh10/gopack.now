---
name: Free app policy
description: Product decision for GoPackNow monetization and release behavior.
---

GoPackNow is fully free for all users. Do not introduce subscription plans, purchase unlocks, paywalls, or in-app purchase SDKs.

**Why:** The creator explicitly decided that every app feature should be available without payment.

**How to apply:** Keep all trip planning, itinerary, packing, and collaboration features accessible without entitlement checks. Avoid adding or configuring RevenueCat or other payment integrations in future mobile builds.
